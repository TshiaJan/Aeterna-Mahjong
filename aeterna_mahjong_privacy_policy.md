# Privacy Policy for Aeterna Mahjong

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026  

Thank you for playing **Aeterna Mahjong** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tshiamo Jantjie. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play our mobile game.

---

## 1. Summary

- **Aeterna Mahjong** does **not** collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
- No user account, registration, or sign-in is required.
- All game progress, level unlocks, earned tokens, and audio/difficulty preferences are saved locally on your device.
- The game can be played fully offline without an active internet connection.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### A. Local Device Storage
Aeterna Mahjong stores minimal, non-personal data directly on your device via local storage (). This includes:
- Highest level reached and current high score
- Virtual token balance (used for hints and optional undos/replays)
- Settings preferences (sound effects, background music volume, and difficulty mode)

This data never leaves your device and is removed if you clear app cache or uninstall the game.

### B. Device & Technical Data
We do not operate backend servers that log your IP address, device serial numbers, or location.

### C. Optional Advertising & Monetization
The game may feature optional rewarded advertisements (such as watching an ad to earn in-game tokens or continue a level). Third-party advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads) may collect anonymized, non-personal device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA) and coarse device metrics solely to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud, in accordance with their respective privacy policies:
- [Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## 3. Children's Privacy

Aeterna Mahjong is designed as a relaxing, family-friendly puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions), in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## 4. Third-Party Links & Services

The game does not integrate external web browsers, social media logins, or external links except for optional standard store interfaces (such as Google Play review prompts).

---

## 5. Data Security

Because we do not transmit or store personal data on remote servers, your gameplay data remains safe on your own physical hardware.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to review this page periodically.
